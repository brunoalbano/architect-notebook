# _sources — AI-generated study briefs

Compressed briefs of dense or scattered docs, **one subfolder per topic** (`_sources/<NN-topic>/<slug>.md`).
Generated by the `source-summarizer` subagent during the `/topic` **Read** phase, or written by hand.

## Rules (every brief must follow)
- Record **source title/URL**, **date summarized**, and a **"⚠️ verify before relying"** note.
- Bias toward tradeoffs: *problem it solves · when NOT to use · failure modes · costs · alternatives ·
  quality attributes affected*. Skip marketing and basics a senior already owns.
- **Never invent** benchmarks, version numbers, API signatures, or limits — tag unverified claims `(verify)`.

These briefs are study inputs, not gospel — they reflect what was true the day they were written.

## ⚖️ Content policy — transformative, not reproductions (this repo may go public)
- **Pointers + your own synthesis, NOT reproductions.** Capture ideas/tradeoffs in your own words and
  **link to the original** for the full text.
- **Do not paste substantial copyrighted text** — no chapter-length summaries, long verbatim quotes, or
  reproduced figures/tables from paywalled books (DDIA, FoSA, Hard Parts…). Short attributed quotes for
  commentary are fine; recreating the book's content is not.
- Official/open docs (Azure, RFCs, vendor blogs) can be summarized more freely — still link & transform.
- When in doubt, write **less** of the source and **more** of your own reasoning.
